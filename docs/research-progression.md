# Research: RPG progression and money for a walking app

2026-10-03. Behind docs/10, *Walks earn XP; rare memories wait for a level*.
Question: replace keys with XP, loot after walks, items with perks and an
inventory, and how that could make money. Primary sources where they exist;
Finch, Orna and Walkr have no public revenue figures.

## What lasted and what didn't

- **Gear that changes what story you get failed.** *Harry Potter: Wizards
  Unite* had layered RPG systems, a capped vault and spell energy. Players
  called it a paywall at launch and it shut in January 2022, $39.4M lifetime
  ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2021/11/02/harry-potter-wizards-unite-shut-down-niantic/),
  [PrimaGames](https://primagames.com/news/harry-potter-wizards-unite-spell-energy-fix-backlash)).
- **Equipment grinds decay.** *Monster Hunter Now*: $219.7M year one, −69% year
  two, −26% year three
  ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/monster-hunter-now-makes-3369m-in-three-years-on-mobile/)).
- **Gentle collections grow.** *Pikmin Bloom* (flowers, postcards, a companion,
  no combat): $8.4M in 2022, $34.8M in 2025, up every year
  ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/pikmin-bloom-hits-100m-after-four-years-as-2025-becomes-its-best-year-yet)).
- **Walking-native mechanics.** Pokémon GO's distance eggs, buddy and Pokédex;
  engaged players walked about 25% more over 30 days
  ([JMIR 2016](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5174727)). Ingress
  never takes medals away, even through a level reset
  ([Ingress](https://support.ingress.com/hc/en-us/articles/41140355729179-Recursion)).
- **Streaks are the best-evidenced lever.** Duolingo: 600+ experiments, daily
  churn down more than 40%
  ([Duolingo](https://blog.duolingo.com/improving-the-streak)).
- **Story carries motivation without stats.** Zombies, Run! moves people with
  chapters, not gear; geocachers name discovery and nature, not rewards
  ([Zombies, Run!](https://blog.zombiesrungame.com/2022/03/30/ten-years-of-zombies-run/)).
- **Cash-value rewards bring in people who leave.** STEPN's daily users fell
  from 58k to 5.8k ([Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/111809/layoffs-hit-struggling-crypto-fitness-app-stepn)).

## Rewards and motivation

- An expected reward for doing something people already enjoy lowers their
  interest in it (128 studies, d ≈ −0.4,
  [Deci, Koestner & Ryan 1999](https://depts.washington.edu/techdocs/papers/deciExtrinsicRewardsAndIntrinsicMotivation99.pdf)).
  An unexpected one does not. So: XP always, and no loot that is owed per walk.
  (Surprise keepsakes shipped and were cut the same day for walk cards: your
  own walks, drawn, are the collection.)
- Points, levels and leaderboards on their own are roughly neutral for
  motivation ([Mekler et al. 2017](https://glassmanlab.seas.harvard.edu/annotated_works/mekler17.pdf)).
  When a reward feels out of place, motivation falls
  ([Diefenbach & Müssig 2019](https://epub.ub.uni-muenchen.de/77668)).

## The WoW question: effort-gated content

The founder's case: Naxx was for those who put the effort in, and that is the
fun. Effort-gating is not what failed above; money- and gear-gating story is.
The caveat is Naxx's own history: few players saw 40-man Naxx, and Blizzard
re-released it in WotLK for everyone. A neighbourhood has a handful of
memories, so the gate is a small rare tier, never a category, and never one
that empties a newcomer's walk.

## Money

| App | Model | Figure |
|---|---|---|
| Pokémon GO | Coins: incubators, raid passes, events | $9B+ lifetime; 2023 remote-raid-pass price rise led to a boycott |
| Pikmin Bloom | Coins and event tickets | $100M lifetime, still growing |
| Duolingo | Subscription first | Q2 2026: $258.0M subscriptions vs $8.0M IAP (IAP −23% YoY) ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026053299/q2fy26duolingo6-30x26share.htm)) |
| Zombies, Run! | Subscription, story | ~50k subs of ~300k MAU |
| Strava, Geocaching | Subscription for convenience | Most revenue from subscriptions |

- Health & Fitness has the best revenue per install of any category: $0.63
  at day 60. Freemium converts 2.18% of installs by day 35, a hard paywall
  12.11%. Annual plans retain 44% at a year, monthly 17%
  ([RevenueCat 2025](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2025/)).
- Paid random items: odds disclosure in both stores (Apple 3.1.1), treated as
  illegal gambling in Belgium, and targeted by the EU Digital Fairness Act and
  China's draft rules. Free drops earned by walking are none of these
  ([Apple](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/),
  [Freshfields](https://technologyquotient.freshfields.com/post/102ltio/the-eus-proposed-digital-fairness-act-a-game-developers-guide-to-potential-imp)).
- Apple 3.1.1: paid unlocks of content must go through in-app purchase. Standing
  at a place is not a payment.

**If and when to charge:** a subscription for convenience (more walks, offline
city packs, narration, walk history), annual first, with the paywall after the
first opened memory. Cosmetics at a shown price, never in random boxes. Never
sell XP, levels or the rare tier.
